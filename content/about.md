**Carollt** combines **Carrot** and **roll**. [Carrot](https://carrot.tw/) is the author's name 🥕, and "roll" means a list or directory.

This is a simple, lightweight index for collecting indie blog discovery spaces and "blogrollrolls" (lists of blogrolls).

## Terms

- **Platform**: Here, "platform" means a community or tool that connects independent blogs—such as directories, webrings, random site hoppers, and RSS aggregators. It does not mean a blog hosting service or a social media network.
- **Blogroll**: A list of other blogs recommended by a blogger, often called "friend links" or "blog friends".
- **Blogrollroll**: A list of blogrolls. The term [blogrollroll](https://blogblog.club/blogrollroll/) seems to have been coined by [Wiwi](https://wiwi.blog/)!
- **Blogrollrollroll**: A list of lists of blogrolls—a word I made up myself😆, with a slight nod to [nownownow](https://nownownow.com/)!I just want to gather as many of these spaces as possible without getting too hung up on tongue-twister definitions🤣

## What Gets Listed

- Brief notes on each space's features, language, and roughly how many sites it includes.
- Categories include directories, webrings, random site hoppers, and RSS aggregators.
